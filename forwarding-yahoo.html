Forwarding from Yahoo Mail

Yahoo Mail forwards all of a mailbox's mail, not letters from one sender, and on many accounts only with a paid plan. If your account doesn't offer Forwarding, subscribe each service with the AlertKeeper address directly instead.

Steps

  1. In Yahoo Mail: Settings → More settings → Mailboxes, choose your address, and under Forwarding type the AlertKeeper address.
  2. Yahoo sends a verification letter to the AlertKeeper mailbox. Sign in to the AlertKeeper Gmail in your browser, open that letter and follow its link.
  3. Everything now arrives at AlertKeeper: on Review, choose "Ignore everything from this address" for mail that isn't from a service.

What you should see

  • Yahoo shows the forwarding address as verified; the services' letters arrive on Alerts.

If it doesn't work

  • No Forwarding option: your Yahoo plan doesn't include it. Subscribe the services with the AlertKeeper address directly.
