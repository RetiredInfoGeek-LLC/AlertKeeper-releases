The Trading Desk pack

Where this comes from

Originator and date

AlertKeeper's maker, 10/14/2026, for a subscriber to Greg Guenthner's The Trading Desk (Paradigm Press) who had made it a service on Review the same day.

Industry sources read

The Trading Desk's own letters, as a subscriber received them (six, scrubbed copies kept outside AlertKeeper's code) -- all commentary: the live chats and the market notes; the orders Paradigm's other alerts write, for its alerts.

What prompted it

Six Trading Desk letters kept out of sight before 10/14: Paradigm's shared address, and no pack claimed the From name "The Trading Desk".

What we changed and why

Its letters are mostly commentary: each is on Alerts by itself, information only, never matched to your trades. An alert (its "Action to take" section) is read for the orders Paradigm writes; one whose order reads otherwise, or a commentary letter with an order in it, waits under Review -- never guessed.

Our own evidence

`tests/test_paradigm_packs.py`: made-up letters in these shapes. On the subscriber's six letters: six commentary letters, no order.
