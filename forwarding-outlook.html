Forwarding from Outlook.com

Outlook forwards with a rule. Choose Redirect: it keeps the service as the sender (Forward makes the letter come from you).

Steps

  1. On outlook.com: Settings (the gear) → Mail → Rules → Add new rule.
  2. Name it after the service. Condition: From — the service's sender address.
  3. Action: Redirect to — the AlertKeeper address (yourname.alerts+servicename@gmail.com). Press Save.
  4. Repeat the rule for each service.

What you should see

  • The service's next letter on AlertKeeper's Alerts.

If it doesn't work

  • Outlook says forwarding isn't allowed: some work and school accounts block it. Subscribe the service with the AlertKeeper address directly instead.
  • The letter shows under Review from your own address: the rule used Forward, not Redirect — change it, or use the service's +word address.
