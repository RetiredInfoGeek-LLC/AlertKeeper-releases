If AlertKeeper doesn't know your broker

Import the broker's transactions file anyway. AlertKeeper shows the first rows and guesses which column is the date, the action, the symbol, the quantity, the price, the fees, the amount and the account, and what each action word means. You check and correct the guesses; anything you're not sure of goes to Review. The answers are kept as your own layout for that broker (Settings → Your own file layouts): used for later files with the same columns, and never replaced by updates.

Last resort: AlertKeeper's own template. Copy your trades into it, one row per action, save it as CSV and import it.

Date
The day it happened, as YYYY-MM-DD.
Account
The masked number your broker shows, like ...123. Optional.
Action
One of: Buy, Sell (shares); Buy to Open, Sell to Open, Buy to Close, Sell to Close; Expired; Assigned; Dividend, Interest, Deposit, Withdrawal, Fee.
Symbol
The ticker, or an option as ticker, expiry, strike and C or P: WXYZ 2026-10-16 20 P.
Quantity
Always positive: shares, or contracts for options.
Amount
Leave it empty on trades and AlertKeeper works it out. On money rows: money into the account positive, money out negative.
