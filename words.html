Words AlertKeeper uses

Service
A newsletter or alert service whose letters AlertKeeper reads: one you pay for, or a free one.
Rec
A service's recommendation: to buy (or sell) a stock or an option, and later its close -- "sell", "close half". A rec stays in force until the service closes it.
Information-only
Lines from a service that doesn't recommend trades to you -- a fund's own trades (ARK), a free letter's picks or ranks (Zacks). Shown grey on Alerts, never matched to your trades, never scored.
Exact and possible match
Exact: your trade is in the very stock or option contract a rec names. Possible: the same underlying stock, but another strike, expiry, or shares instead of an option (or the other way). A possible match counts for the service only once you confirm it under Review.
Position
What you hold now in one stock or one option contract, in one account.
Trade
One position from its first buy (or sell, for a short) until it's back to zero. Buying again later starts a new trade.
Lot
Each purchase (or sale, for a short) inside a trade. When you sell part, the oldest lot goes first.
Realized P/L
Money made or lost on what you've closed: what you received less what you paid, fees included. Options count 100 shares each.
Starting position
Something you held before your imported history begins. Its cost comes from your positions file or from you, and it's always labelled.
Manual
A trade you entered yourself (Trades → Enter a trade manually) because your broker's files don't show it yet. It counts like any fill, marked Manual in amber, until an imported file carries the same fill: then the file's rows replace it and the mark goes.
Paper account
Practice money: trades you type in (or start from a rec), with no broker and no number. Its results are always shown apart from your real accounts'.
Transferred in
Shares or options moved into the account from another. Their cost comes from your broker's lot details or positions file, and they're labelled too.
Review
Anything AlertKeeper wasn't sure of. It's listed with the rows involved instead of being guessed.
Mark
An option's price saved one evening after the market closed: the middle of the bid and ask, or the last trade. A day without one says "no price — not scored", never a guess.
DTE
Days to expiry: on Positions, the calendar days to an option contract's last trading day -- its expiry, or the trading day before it when the expiry is a market holiday. "today" on that day; under 7 days it shows amber. Positions are listed by it, soonest first, shares last.
Unrealized P/L
What an open position would bring at its latest price, less what it cost. Nothing is sold, so it isn't counted as made or lost. On Positions: Value less Cost, in dollars and as a percent of the cost paid ("+$482.00 (+6.2%)").
Day
On Positions, how much a position's value changed since the previous saved price, for the latest market session, in dollars and as a percent of the cost paid. Until that session's close (shares) or evening mark (options) is saved it shows —, never an older day's change.
Settled and working closes
On Exit Scorecard a close is settled once its reference is known: the service's exit, or the contract's last day once it has passed. Until then it's working, with a reading so far. Its realized P/L is final at the close either way.
Ahead, Behind, Followed, Late
Your close against the service's exit: well before it and better or worse, on it (its day, the day before, or just after), or after it.
Right call, Early
A close of your own, against the contract's last day: right when holding on would have left you no better off, early when it would have done better. Near flat closes aren't judged.
Colours
On every page: green, all fine; grey, something to know, nothing to do; amber, needs you -- something is owed, and the card says what to do; red, needs you now. The dot in the menu bar (on a Mac) or the tray (on Windows) shows the worst colour (grey leaves it green).
Pack
The part of AlertKeeper that knows one service's letters, or one broker's files, or the market's calendar. Packs come with AlertKeeper and are updated with it; each is checked against a signed fingerprint before it's used.

ARK Invest and Zacks are examples of letters AlertKeeper can read, not services we suggest or endorse. AlertKeeper recommends no service.
