The Income Alliance pack

Where this comes from

Originator and date

AlertKeeper's maker, 10/14/2026, for a subscriber to Zach Scheidt's The Income Alliance (Paradigm Press, its "Income Matrix" option trades) who had made it a service on Review the same day.

Industry sources read

The Income Alliance's own letters, as a subscriber received them (three Income Matrix alerts, scrubbed copies kept outside AlertKeeper's code); Zach Scheidt's True Alpha pack (October 9) for a sale of half a position.

What prompted it

Two Income Matrix alerts of 10/13 (taking gains, adjusting a position) and one of 10/12, kept out of sight before 10/14: Paradigm's shared address, and no pack claimed the From name "The Income Alliance".

What we changed and why

Each alert is usually a roll: the old contracts sold ("Sell your 5 ... calls using a limit order of $..." -- a close of that contract, its limit the least to sell for) and new ones bought ("Buy 5 ... calls using a limit order of $..." -- its limit the most to pay). The number of contracts is the editor's own and isn't kept. Commentary letters are on Alerts by themselves, information only.

Our own evidence

`tests/test_paradigm_packs.py`: made-up letters in these shapes. On the subscriber's three letters: three rolls, each the old calls closed with the least to sell for and new calls bought with the most to pay -- six orders, each with its contract.
