Your Fidelity files

Two files per account: the activity (your trades) and the positions. AlertKeeper's Fidelity reading is built from published descriptions and waits for its first real file: anything it doesn't know goes to Needs a look.

Steps

  1. On fidelity.com: Accounts & Trade → Activity & Orders, choose the account, set the dates, then Download (CSV). A download of several accounts is fine: AlertKeeper splits it by account.
  2. Accounts & Trade → Portfolio → Positions → Download.
  3. In AlertKeeper, Import: drop all the files on the page at once. Choose or make the account for each.

What you should see

  • Before saving, the count of trades and other rows; then your positions on Positions.

If it doesn't work

  • Rows under Review: a wording AlertKeeper hasn't seen from Fidelity yet. They're kept; tell the AlertKeeper team what they say.
  • Fidelity's realized gain/loss download isn't read yet.
